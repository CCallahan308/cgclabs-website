Practice 03 · Data engineering

Old data, made usable.

I extract historical data from the systems that hold it, clean it, classify it, and structure it for machine learning and AI training. The archive becomes a dataset. The dataset becomes work.

The problem

The data exists. It is just unusable.

Years of records sit in old databases, exports, and flat files nothing modern reads. Nothing is classified, so nothing can train a model, and the AI project stalls before it starts.

  • Records scattered across formats, systems, and years
  • No shared schema, so the same field means three things
  • Unlabeled data a model cannot learn from
  • No pipeline to move anything anywhere
What I do

From archive to training set.

01

Extraction

Historical records pulled out of legacy systems, exports, and paper-era flat files, with the source system left intact.

02

Cleaning and normalization

Deduplication, schema mapping, and reconciliation of conflicting definitions, so one record means one thing everywhere.

03

Classification for training

Records labeled and structured for machine learning and AI work, with the labeling rules written down so the dataset is reproducible and auditable.

04

Delivery and pipeline

Load into the warehouse or the environment your project runs in, on a pipeline that refreshes on schedule and alerts when it does not.

05

Documentation

Lineage from source to dataset. Anyone can trace where a field came from and why it looks the way it does.

The standard
