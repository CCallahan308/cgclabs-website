The gap

You get the number every month. You get no reason behind it.

The board sees a red flag and asks why. The answer takes a week, arrives as a spreadsheet, and settles nothing. The problem is not effort. It is that nobody agreed on what the number means, so nobody can say what moves it.

What leadership sees

  • A red flag with no drill-down below it
  • Three departments, three definitions of the same metric
  • An enterprise dashboard nobody opens
  • Reporting that consumes the month and decides nothing

What I build

  • One view per audience, built for the meeting it gets read in
  • Every number drills to the department, the driver, and the date

Method

The method is named, documented, and old enough to trust.

The practice runs on public industry standards, applied the way an operator applies them. The Balanced Scorecard carries four perspectives: financial, patient, internal process, learning and growth. CRISP-DM is the six-phase delivery lifecycle, which is why a working dashboard lands inside 30 days. Parmenter's measure discipline keeps the register honest, because most KPIs are not KPIs and the few that are get acted on now. Benchmarks come from the healthcare peer sets your quality team already reports against: HCAHPS, CMS Care Compare, and MBQIP.
