Legal

Privacy statement.

The short version: this site collects nothing. If you email me, I have your email. That is the whole pipeline.

What this site is

cgclabs.org is a static website. It has no accounts, no comment sections, no contact forms, no newsletter, and no checkout. Every button that looks like an action opens your own email client with the address filled in. Nothing is submitted to this site.

What I collect

The site itself collects nothing. No analytics scripts run here. No advertising trackers are present. I do not profile visitors, and I do not know who you are unless you tell me.

Email

If you email christian.g.callahan@cgclabs.org, I receive your email address and whatever you wrote. I use it to reply to you and to do the work you asked about. I keep correspondence only as long as the conversation or engagement needs it. I do not add you to a mailing list, and I do not share or sell your address.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard request data (your IP address, the page requested, the time) in order to deliver pages and protect against abuse. I do not buy, mine, or cross-reference those logs. Vercel's own privacy notice describes its handling: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

Fonts

Typefaces load from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files directly from Google's servers, which sees the request as part of delivery. No font script tracks you around this site.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. The cookie statement has the detail.

Your information, on request

You can ask me to delete any correspondence I hold from you. Email me and I will do it, and confirm when it is done. There is no database to purge, so the answer is quick by construction.

Changes

If the site ever gains a form, an analytics tool, or any data collection, I will rewrite this statement before that goes live and note the date here. Last updated: September 29, 2026.
