Cookie statement.

The short version: this site sets no cookies, so there is nothing to accept and no banner in your way.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store, usually to remember you between visits or to track what you do. Some are needed for a site to work. Many exist to measure or advertise.

What this site sets

Nothing. cgclabs.org is a static site with no accounts, no shopping cart, no comment forms, and no analytics. There is nothing to remember and nothing to measure, so no cookies are set. No consent banner is needed for the same reason.

Local storage and similar tech

The site writes nothing to your browser's local storage, session storage, or indexedDB. The small amount of JavaScript on the page handles the menu and the number animations, and it stores nothing.

Third parties that see requests

Two services touch your browser when you load a page, neither through cookies set by this site.

Vercel hosts and serves the pages. It processes standard request data to deliver them. Google Fonts serves the typefaces; your browser fetches the font files from Google's servers. Neither is a tracker I installed, and neither needs a cookie from this site to work.

If that changes

If the site ever adds something that sets a cookie, this statement will be rewritten first, with the date noted, and a banner will appear only if the law actually requires one. Last updated: September 29, 2026.
